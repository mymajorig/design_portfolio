About

I’m a UI/UX designer and creative technologist driven by curiosity and a passion for exploring how design can create meaningful change. I’m interested in the intersection of design, technology, and storytelling through using research, visual thinking, and code to rethink how people interact with products and ideas.

I’m drawn to design because it combines creativity, human understanding, and problem-solving. I enjoy exploring how thoughtful experiences can simplify complexity, build trust, and extend beyond interfaces to influence strategy, communication, and emerging technologies. As I continue to grow, I hope to create work that not only solves problems but also leaves a lasting impact: helping people connect with products in ways that are thoughtful, engaging, and distinctly human. I’m always excited to connect with others who are passionate about design, technology, and creating meaningful experiences!
